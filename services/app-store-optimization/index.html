What the ASO engagement produces

  • A query map grouped by intent, market, language and current rank
  • Apple title, subtitle and keyword-field recommendations within current limits
  • Google Play title, short description and long-description recommendations
  • Category and competitor review based on actual search results
  • A screenshot narrative, caption brief and icon test hypotheses
  • Localization priorities based on demand and conversion opportunity
  • A release-ready metadata sheet with character counts and source evidence
  • A measurement plan for impressions, product-page views, installs and paid conversion

The work can end as a strategy package or continue into implementation and store submission. Scope depends on the number of stores, languages and creative variants.

How keyword research works

For Apple, we use Apple Ads Search Popularity as the primary first-party demand signal where available. We pair it with live App Store results, the strength and relevance of ranking apps, estimated downloads, ratings, review language and the product's current impression data.

For Google Play, we combine search suggestions, live result intent, third-party directional estimates and the app's Play Console acquisition data. A third-party volume number is a hypothesis, not proof that the app can rank or that the traffic will convert.

Discovery intentThe user knows the problem or desired outcome but not the app. These terms can create incremental reach.
Category and feature intentThe user compares a recognizable type of app or a capability. Relevance and proof matter more than repetition.
Brand and competitor intentUseful for defense or comparison only when the listing makes the relationship clear and follows store policy.

Metadata and creative serve different jobs

Metadata helps the store understand relevance. The icon, first screenshots, preview video, rating and review evidence help the visitor decide. We do not hide the product behind abstract captions or optimize for a phrase the signed build cannot deliver.

  • The first screenshot explains the main outcome immediately
  • Each following frame answers a distinct buying objection
  • Interface evidence remains legible on a phone
  • Claims match the current production version
  • Localization adapts the promise and visual order, not only the text
  • Paid and organic creative tests inform the next listing release

Measurement and decision rules

We measure the funnel by store, country, source and version: search impression, product-page view, install, activation and paid outcome where available. A rank increase without qualified installs is not success. A conversion increase on a tiny traffic base may not be statistically useful.

The first cycle establishes a baseline and ships the highest-confidence metadata or creative change. Later cycles isolate one meaningful hypothesis at a time using Product Page Optimization, Custom Product Pages, Google Play experiments or controlled release comparisons where appropriate.

What ASO does not promise

No provider controls store ranking or can guarantee an installation volume. Product quality, retention, reviews, paid acquisition, brand demand and platform behavior all affect the result. ASO is strongest when it sharpens a product users already understand and want.
