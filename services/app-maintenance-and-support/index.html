Maintenance baseline

The first month establishes what can be operated safely. We reproduce the build from a clean checkout, verify account ownership, identify production services, review current store status and connect release health to a specific version.

  • Source, cloud and store access are owned by the business
  • Build and signing steps are documented and reproducible
  • Development, staging and production are distinguishable
  • Crashes and ANRs identify version, platform and affected users
  • Critical product journeys have success and controlled failure signals
  • Dependencies, target OS versions and store deadlines are inventoried
  • Backup, rollback and feature-disable options are known
  • Support and incident escalation owners are explicit

The operating loop

  1. Review release health, store notices, dependency risk and support patterns.
  2. Prioritize by user impact, deadline and likelihood rather than by raw alert count.
  3. Agree on the bounded maintenance or release scope.
  4. Implement, test and distribute through the appropriate testing track.
  5. Watch the same signals after rollout and record the result.
  6. Turn escaped failures into a check, monitor or runbook for the next release.

The mobile app monitoring guide explains the signals we expect, while the mobile app launching checklist shows the release gates reused on each cycle.

Included and separately scoped work

Normal maintenance includes bounded dependency updates, store policy work, build failures, configuration changes, crash triage and agreed release coordination. New product features, redesigns, backend migrations, major framework upgrades, security incidents and around-the-clock response require a separate scope.

You keep the repositories, accounts, credentials and release history. App Release Ops operates the process; it does not make the product dependent on a private maintenance platform.

Good fit

This service fits a founder, small product team or agency with a live or near-live web, iOS or Android product and no dedicated release-operations owner. If the current build is not reproducible or the production risk is unknown, begin with the production readiness audit. If the immediate problem is distribution, use the App Store publishing or Google Play publishing service.
