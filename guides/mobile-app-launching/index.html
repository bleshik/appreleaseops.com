Mobile app launching checklist

Mobile app launching is a production, distribution and operations project—not a single upload.

Reviewed by Aleksei BalchunasProduct founder · web, iOS and Android releases
On this pageProduction readinessStore readinessReal-device testingControlled rolloutThe first week

Production readiness

Verify authentication, account deletion, secrets, database permissions, payments, privacy, analytics, crash reporting and support paths.

Store readiness

Prepare signed builds, listings, screenshots, age and content declarations, data disclosures, review credentials and release notes.

Real-device testing

Cover slow networks, interrupted auth, denied permissions, offline behavior, restore purchases and upgrade from the previous version.

Controlled rollout

Choose manual or staged release, monitor crashes and conversion, keep a rollback-ready build and assign an owner for reviewer communication.

The first week

Watch support, ratings, purchase failures, ANRs and crashes. Ship the first corrective release through the same documented process.
