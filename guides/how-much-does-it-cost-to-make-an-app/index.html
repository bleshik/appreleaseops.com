How much does it cost to make an app?

The useful answer depends on what already exists and which production responsibilities the app must own.

Reviewed by Aleksei BalchunasProduct founder · web, iOS and Android releases
On this pageExisting website to store-ready wrapperCross-platform custom mobile appNative iOS and AndroidBudget for operations

Existing website to store-ready wrapper

For a responsive product with limited native needs, a managed packaging and publishing project can start around $1,500–$3,000. Payments, offline behavior and complex device APIs increase scope.

Cross-platform custom mobile app

A focused Expo, React Native or Flutter build commonly starts in the five figures once product design, backend work, QA and both stores are included.

Native iOS and Android

Separate native codebases cost more to build and maintain. Choose them when platform-specific capability or performance creates measurable value.

Budget for operations

Developer accounts, infrastructure, monitoring, support, store compliance and repeated releases remain after launch. A low initial build price does not remove those costs.
