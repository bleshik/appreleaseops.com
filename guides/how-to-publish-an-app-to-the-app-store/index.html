How to publish an app to the App Store

The complete path from an existing app build to an Apple-reviewed release.

Release checklist1. Create the right Apple account2. Configure identifiers and signing3. Prepare the product listing4. Complete privacy and compliance5. Test in TestFlight6. Submit with useful review notes
Step 1

Create the right Apple account

Enroll as an individual or organization, confirm legal details and give the release operator the correct App Store Connect role.

Step 2

Configure identifiers and signing

Set the bundle ID, capabilities, certificates and provisioning so local, TestFlight and production builds are reproducible.

Step 3

Prepare the product listing

Write the name, subtitle, description and keywords; produce device-correct screenshots and complete age rating and support URLs.

Step 4

Complete privacy and compliance

Map every collected data type, SDK and tracking behavior to Apple’s privacy form. Add export compliance and content rights answers.

Step 5

Test in TestFlight

Exercise onboarding, auth, purchases, failure states and real-device layout. Add external testers when the risk warrants it.

Step 6

Submit with useful review notes

Give reviewers test credentials, explain non-obvious flows and respond quickly to questions or rejection reasons.
